KSS: Engineering Trustworthy AI — From System Architecture to Verifiable Evidence

This session builds a complete mental model of how trustworthy AI systems are engineered — not as a checklist of principles, but as a defense-in-depth architecture where every layer (input guardrails, output validation, tool authorization, governance) is an independently testable safety checkpoint. Using one persistent case study (DocuBot, an enterprise AI agent processing confidential documents), we trace the causal chain from capability to failure to control to evidence to governance, cover all mandatory topics from prompt injection to data poisoning to alignment, and close by mapping every claim against our actual codebase — including an explicit accounting of what we have not yet built. Technical depth, precise vocabulary, no marketing.
